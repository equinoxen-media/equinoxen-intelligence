# Claim Audit: Best Accounting Software for Small Business 2026: QuickBooks vs FreshBooks vs Zoho Books

**URL:** https://equinoxen.com/best-accounting-software-small-business-2026/
**Audited:** 2026-08-29

**Summary:** 9 claims checked — 3 confirmed, 2 discrepancies, 3 outdated, 1 unverifiable

## 🔴 Discrepancies (fix these first)
- **QuickBooks Online** (price): "Essentials tier costs $65/month"
  - Found: The current official price for QuickBooks Online Essentials is $85 per month as of August 2026, not $65 per month. The price increased from $75/month to $85/month effective August 1, 2026.
  - Source: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/product-update/quickbooks-price-changes/
- **QuickBooks Online** (price): "Advanced tier costs $235/month"
  - Found: According to current official pricing information, QuickBooks Online Advanced costs $275 per month, not $235 per month as claimed.
  - Source: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/pricing/

## 🟡 Possibly Outdated
- **QuickBooks Online** (price): "Simple Start tier costs $35/month"
  - Found: QuickBooks Online Simple Start currently costs $38 per month, not $35. The price increased from $35 to $38 as part of Intuit's pricing changes that took effect in mid-2025 and apply to 2026 subscriptions.
  - Source: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/compare/square-vs-quickbooks/
- **QuickBooks Online** (price): "Plus tier costs $99/month"
  - Found: The Plus tier now costs $140/month as of August 2026, according to multiple current sources including vendor-related pricing information. The claim of $99/month was the previous price before the August 2026 price increase.
  - Source: https://www.certumsolutions.com/library/quickbooks-online-plans-compared
- **QuickBooks Online** (price): "Payroll services cost an additional $45/month plus $5 per employee"
  - Found: Current QuickBooks Workforce Payroll pricing is $50/month plus $6.50 per employee as of July 2026, not $45/month plus $5 per employee as claimed. The product was also rebranded from QuickBooks Online Payroll to QuickBooks Workforce.
  - Source: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/payroll/pricing/

## ⚪ Unverifiable
- **QuickBooks Online** (other): "Automated transaction categorization correctly classifies roughly 95% of expenses without manual intervention"
  - Intuit's official documentation states QuickBooks Online can auto-categorize expenses with a 'high rate of accuracy' but does not specify a 95% accuracy figure. Third-party sources cite 85-95% or 50% accuracy depending on context, but no official Intuit pages confirm the specific 95% claim.
