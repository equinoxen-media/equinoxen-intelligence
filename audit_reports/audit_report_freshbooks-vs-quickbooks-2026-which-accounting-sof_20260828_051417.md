# Claim Audit: FreshBooks vs QuickBooks 2026: Which Accounting Software Wins

**URL:** https://equinoxen.com/freshbooks-vs-quickbooks/
**Audited:** 2026-08-28

**Summary:** 19 claims checked — 10 confirmed, 6 discrepancies, 2 outdated, 1 unverifiable

## 🔴 Discrepancies (fix these first)
- **FreshBooks** (price): "Lite plan costs $19 per month"
  - Found: Current official pricing shows the Lite plan costs $23 per month when billed monthly, not $19. Multiple sources verified from FreshBooks' official pricing page show $23/month as of mid-2026, though the price was $19/month in early 2025.
  - Source: https://www.freshbooks.com/pricing
- **FreshBooks** (price): "Plus plan costs $33 per month"
  - Found: The FreshBooks Plus plan currently costs $43 per month, not $33 per month. Multiple sources verified this pricing from FreshBooks' official website as of mid-2026, and one source noted that pricing increased from $33/month in early 2025 to $43/month.
  - Source: https://www.freshbooks.com/pricing
- **FreshBooks** (price): "Premium tier costs $60 per month"
  - Found: The FreshBooks Premium tier currently costs $70 per month when billed monthly, not $60 per month. Multiple sources verified against the official FreshBooks pricing page confirm this rate as of 2026.
  - Source: https://www.freshbooks.com/pricing
- **QuickBooks Online** (integration): "Connects with over 20,000 financial institutions"
  - Found: Current evidence suggests QuickBooks Online connects to approximately 2,936 U.S. financial institutions, not over 20,000. Official QuickBooks documentation does not cite the 20,000 figure.
  - Source: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/global/bank-feeds/
- **QuickBooks Online** (price): "Simple Start plan costs $35 per month"
  - Found: The current official pricing for QuickBooks Online Simple Start is $38 per month, not $35 per month as claimed.
  - Source: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/pricing/
- **QuickBooks Online** (price): "Essentials plan costs $65 per month"
  - Found: The current official price for QuickBooks Online Essentials is $85 per month as of August 1, 2026, following a price increase from the previous $75/month rate. The claimed price of $65/month does not match current vendor pricing.
  - Source: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/product-update/quickbooks-price-changes/
