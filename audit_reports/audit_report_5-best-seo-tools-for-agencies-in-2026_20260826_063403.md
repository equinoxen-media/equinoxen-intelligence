# Claim Audit: 5 Best SEO Tools for Agencies in 2026

**URL:** https://equinoxen.com/best-seo-tools-for-agencies/
**Audited:** 2026-08-26

**Summary:** 21 claims checked — 11 confirmed, 8 discrepancies, 2 outdated, 0 unverifiable

## 🔴 Discrepancies (fix these first)
- **Semrush** (plan_limit): "Position Tracking supports up to 20,000 keywords"
  - Found: According to Semrush's official documentation, Position Tracking supports up to 5,000 keywords on the Business plan, not 20,000. The keyword limits are: Pro (500), Guru (1,500), and Business (5,000).
  - Source: https://semrush.com/kb/696-what-are-the-limits-of-my-position-tracking-campaign
- **Semrush** (price): "Pro plan starts at $129.95/month"
  - Found: Current official Semrush documentation and multiple sources show the Pro plan at $139.95 per month for monthly billing, not $129.95 as claimed in the statement.
  - Source: https://it.semrush.com/kb/1547-seo-toolkit-pricing-limits
- **Ahrefs** (other): "Has 36 trillion backlinks in the index"
  - Found: Ahrefs' official pages currently report 35 trillion external backlinks in their index, not 36 trillion as claimed.
  - Source: https://ahrefs.com/backlink-checker
- **Ahrefs** (plan_limit): "Site Audit can crawl up to 500,000 pages"
  - Found: According to Ahrefs' current official pricing page and documentation, the Standard plan provides 500,000 crawl credits per month (not a per-crawl limit of 500,000 pages). The claim conflates monthly crawl credit allocation with per-crawl page limits, which are separate settings in Site Audit.
  - Source: https://ahrefs.com/pricing
- **Ahrefs** (other): "Keywords Explorer has 171 million keyword database across 10 search engines"
  - Found: Ahrefs currently reports 28.7 billion keywords across 217 locations, not 171 million keywords across 10 search engines. The claim appears to be severely outdated or confused with other metrics.
  - Source: https://ahrefs.com/big-data/
- **Ahrefs** (price): "Advanced plan costs $399/month"
  - Found: The Advanced plan costs $449/month when billed monthly, not $399/month. The $399/month price only applies to annual billing commitments.
  - Source: https://ahrefs.com/pricing
- **Ahrefs** (price): "Enterprise plan costs $999/month"
  - Found: Current sources from 2026 consistently report the Ahrefs Enterprise plan costs $1,499 per month, not $999 per month. The $999 figure appears in one source as part of a range ($999-$1,499) but the actual starting Enterprise price is $1,499.
  - Source: https://ahrefs.com/pricing
- **Ahrefs** (plan_limit): "Enterprise plan includes unlimited projects"
  - Found: The Enterprise plan includes 100 unverified projects and unlimited verified projects, not unlimited projects outright. Verified projects require ownership verification through Ahrefs's process.
  - Source: https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/Notice/Attachment/4e13af92-0ee3-49ae-a842-781c82d62d92

## 🟡 Possibly Outdated
- **Ahrefs** (price): "Lite plan costs $99/month"
  - Found: The Lite plan currently costs $129/month, not $99/month. Ahrefs increased prices in April 2024, raising the Lite plan from $99 to $129 per month.
  - Source: https://ahrefs.com/pricing
- **Ahrefs** (price): "Standard plan costs $199/month"
  - Found: The current Ahrefs Standard plan costs $249/month according to official sources, not $199/month. Ahrefs' own help documentation confirms that $199 was the legacy Standard plan price before it increased to $249.
  - Source: https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/9720420-what-is-my-subscription-plan-how-can-i-check-what-i-have
