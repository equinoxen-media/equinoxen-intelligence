## 🔴 Discrepancies (fix these first)
- **Constant Contact** (feature): "Provides over 200 customizable templates"
  - Found: Constant Contact's official website currently states they offer 600+ professional email templates, not over 200 as claimed. A third-party review from 2026 mentions 200+ templates but the vendor's own site shows a much higher number.
  - Source: https://www.constantcontact.com/features/email-templates
- **Constant Contact** (price): "Standard plan costs $290 per month for 10,000 contacts"
  - Found: Current sources from 2026 consistently report that Constant Contact's Standard plan costs approximately $160 per month for 10,000 contacts, not $290. The $290 figure appears closer to Premium plan pricing at that contact level.
  - Source: https://www.constantcontact.com/pricing
- **Constant Contact** (feature): "Offers a 14-day free trial"
  - Found: Constant Contact currently offers a 30-day free trial, not a 14-day trial. This is confirmed across multiple official Constant Contact pages including their pricing page and blog posts from July-August 2026.
  - Source: https://www.constantcontact.com/pricing
- **Constant Contact** (feature): "Standard plan adds automation, dynamic content, and A/B testing"
  - Found: The Standard plan includes automation and A/B testing, but dynamic content is a Premium plan feature, not Standard. Constant Contact's official pricing page confirms dynamic content is only available on the Premium plan ($80/month).
  - Source: https://www.constantcontact.com/pricing
